# Browsing and Finding Toys

Topic: Using the catalogue page to search, filter and sort toys, and what a product page shows.
Source: /catalog and /product/:slug

## How do I see all the toys?

Choose "Shop all" in the header menu or "Shop all toys" on the home page. The catalogue page lists all 24 toys, 12 per page over 2 pages, with Previous, 1, 2 and Next buttons at the bottom. A summary line such as "24 toys match your filters" shows how many toys fit the current filters.

## How do I search for a toy by name?

Type in the Search box on the catalogue page (it suggests "Blocks, telescope, plush..."). For example, searching "robot" with the price sorted low to high finds the CodeBot Screen-Free Coding Robot. With only one match the summary line reads "1 toys match your filters"; that wording is a small cosmetic quirk and the result is correct.

## How do I filter toys by category?

Use the Category dropdown on the catalogue page. Choices are All categories, Building & Construction, Dolls & Plush, Games & Puzzles, Outdoor & Active Play, and STEM & Learning. The STEM and Outdoor links in the header menu jump straight to those two categories.

## How do I find toys for my child's age?

Use the "Child's age" dropdown on the catalogue page. Choices are Any age, 0-18 months, 18 months-3 years, 3-5 years, 5-8 years and 8+ years. On the home page, "Find by age" opens the catalogue filtered for a 4-year-old (48 months). Each toy also lists its own recommended age range, such as "3 yr - 7 yr", so always check the product page.

## How do I filter by price?

Type a Minimum price and/or a Maximum price in US dollars in the Price boxes on the catalogue page. To see the cheapest toys first, set Sort to "Price: low to high". The footer link "Best value" does this too.

## How do I filter by brand?

Use the Brand dropdown on the catalogue page. The brands are Blockworks, BrightBuild, Fox & Feather, Hollow Hill, Little Lane, Maple & Moss, Northlight, Rove, Sunbeam Play, Tabletop Junior and Tinker Crew.

## How do I show only toys that are in stock?

Tick the "In stock only" checkbox on the catalogue page.

## How can I sort the toys?

Use the Sort dropdown on the catalogue page. Options are Newest first, Price: low to high, Price: high to low, Name: A-Z and Name: Z-A.

## How do I clear my filters?

Press the "Reset" button at the top of the Filters panel on the catalogue page.

## What does a toy card on the catalogue show?

Each card shows the category, the age range, the name, a short description, the price, the stock count (for example "92 in stock") and an "Add to cart" button. Clicking the picture or the name opens the full product page.

## What information is on a product page?

A product page shows the brand, name, price, recommended age, availability (how many are in stock), a description, and safety notes. It also has a quantity box and an "Add to cart" button, and a breadcrumb link back to the catalogue and the toy's category. For example, the Stargazer Beginner Telescope by Northlight costs $94.99, is recommended for ages 8 to 16 years, and its safety note warns never to look at the sun through it.

## Where do I find safety information for a toy?

Every product page has a Safety notes section from the manufacturer. Small parts are a choking hazard for children under three, so check the notes and the recommended age before buying.

## Where can I find details on a specific toy such as its price or stock?

The full list of toys with prices, ages, brands and stock is in the product list. For the live price and stock of any toy, open its product page from the catalogue.
