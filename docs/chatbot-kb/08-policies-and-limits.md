# Policies and Assistant Limits

Topic: What ToyBox does and does not publish as policy, and what the shopping assistant cannot do.
Source: Whole site (footer, checkout pages, Feedback page)

## What are ToyBox's returns and refund policies?

ToyBox has no published returns policy; use the Feedback page. ToyBox also publishes no refund policy, no exchange policy and no warranty terms. The assistant will not make one up.

## Is ToyBox a real store? Are payments real?

ToyBox is a demo store. Checkout uses Stripe in test mode, so no real card is charged and no real toys are shipped. The footer states: "Demo store - payments run in Stripe test mode."

## What can the assistant not do?

The assistant cannot:
- Look up an order or tell you where an order is or its status.
- Change, cancel or fix an order.
- Issue refunds or process returns.
- Create, change or recover accounts, passwords or sign-in details.
- Access or change anything in a shopper's cart, account or payment details.
- Help with the store's back-office or management tools.
- Give advice that is not about ToyBox shopping.
For all of these, use the Feedback page.

## Can the assistant help with admin or store management features?

No. Store management is not something the assistant can help with. Please use the Feedback page for anything of that kind.

## What if I forgot my password?

The site shows no password-recovery option. The assistant cannot reset a password or recover an account; use the Feedback page. A signed-in shopper who still knows the current password can change it from the Account page.

## Are the toys safe for my child?

Every toy lists its manufacturer age range and safety notes on its product page. Small parts are a choking hazard for children under three, so always read the notes before buying. The assistant does not give medical or safety guarantees beyond what the product page states.

## What card details does ToyBox keep?

Card details are entered on Stripe's payment page, not on ToyBox, so ToyBox does not keep card numbers or security codes. The order keeps the name, shipping address and contact email entered at checkout. The assistant itself does not handle card data. Never type a real card number, password or personal secret into the chat; use the standard test card 4242 4242 4242 4242 on the payment page.

## What is the limit on how much I can order?

The quantity of a toy in the cart cannot exceed the number in stock. If it does, the cart shows "Only N left - reduce the quantity to check out." ToyBox publishes no other order limits.

## Does ToyBox have a privacy policy, terms of service, or contact phone number?

None are published on the site. Questions can go to the Feedback page.

## What should I do if a page shows "That shelf is empty"?

That is the 404 page, shown when an address does not exist. Press "Browse the toys" to go back to the catalogue.
