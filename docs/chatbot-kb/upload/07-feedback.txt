# Feedback Page

Topic: How to contact ToyBox with feedback, problems and requests the assistant cannot handle.
Source: /feedback

## How do I contact ToyBox or send feedback?

Choose "Feedback" in the header menu. The page says "Tell us about your experience shopping with ToyBox." and contains an embedded feedback form. Fill in the form and submit it there.

## Where do I go with a problem, complaint or request the assistant cannot handle?

Use the Feedback page. It is the only published way to reach ToyBox, and it is the right place for order questions, receipt problems, refund or return requests, account problems and general comments.

## What fields does the feedback form have?

The form is provided by a separate service embedded in the page, so its fields can change. Open the Feedback page to see the current form.

## Why does the Feedback page say "Feedback form is not configured"?

That message means the form's address has not been set up on this copy of the site, so no form can be shown. Nothing the shopper did caused it.

## Can I get a reply to my feedback?

ToyBox does not publish a response time or a promise of a reply. Include your contact details in the form if you want to be contacted.

## Is there a phone number or support email?

No phone number or support email is published on the site. The Feedback page is the contact route.
