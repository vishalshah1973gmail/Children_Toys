# Shopping Cart

Topic: Adding, changing and removing items in the cart, and what the cart page shows.
Source: /cart (and "Add to cart" buttons on /catalog, the home page and product pages)

## How do I add a toy to my cart?

Press "Add to cart" on a toy card or on its product page. On a product page, set the quantity first if you want more than one. You do not need to sign in: the cart works before you sign in, and the Cart link in the header shows a number badge once it has items.

## How do I change the quantity of an item in my cart?

Open the Cart page from the header, then edit the number in the quantity box on that item's line. The quantity cannot go above the number in stock.

## How do I remove an item from my cart?

On the Cart page, press "Remove" on that item's line. To remove everything at once, press "Empty cart" at the top of the page.

## What does the cart page show?

Each line shows the product picture and name (both open the product page), the price each, a quantity box, a Remove button and the line total. An Order summary shows Subtotal, Shipping, Estimated tax and Total.

## What does "Only N left - reduce the quantity to check out" mean?

The quantity in the cart is higher than the stock available for that toy. Lower the quantity to the number shown, or less, and checkout becomes available.

## Do I need an account to use the cart?

Not to add items. Checking out needs an account: register, wait for administrator approval, then sign in. The items in the guest cart are merged into the account cart the first time the shopper signs in after approval, so nothing is lost.

## What are the checkout buttons on the cart page?

When signed out, the cart page shows "Sign in to check out" (which takes you to sign in and then on to checkout) and a note to log in or register, with a "Create an account" link. When signed in, the button reads "Proceed to checkout".

## How are the shipping, tax and total in my cart worked out?

Shipping is a flat $5.99 when the subtotal is under $50.00 and free at $50.00 or more. Tax is 6.63% of the subtotal only. The total is subtotal plus shipping plus tax. The server recalculates everything from current prices before payment, so the numbers on the page are always confirmed by the store.

## Are prices and stock guaranteed while items sit in my cart?

No. Prices and stock are confirmed on the server before payment is taken, so a total can change if a price or stock level has changed since the item was added.
