# ToyBox Store Overview

Topic: What ToyBox is, what it sells, and where to find things on the site.
Source: Home page (/) and the site header and footer.

## What is ToyBox?

ToyBox is an online toy store for children ages 0 to 14. Every toy lists its real manufacturer age range, its brand and its safety notes, so shoppers can buy for the child in front of them. The store's tagline is "Toys worth keeping."

## Is ToyBox a real store? Will my card be charged?

ToyBox is a demo store. Account checkout uses Stripe in test mode, so no real money moves, and the test card 4242 4242 4242 4242 (any future expiry, any security code) is the card to use. Guest checkout is a simulated payment: it checks the card details but never contacts a real card processor and never makes a real charge. Do not expect real toys to ship.

## What kinds of toys does ToyBox sell?

ToyBox sells toys in five categories. The exact number of toys in each category changes as the catalogue changes; the product list or the live catalogue page has the current numbers.
- Building & Construction: blocks, bricks, magnetic tiles and marble runs.
- Dolls & Plush: soft companions, dolls and playsets.
- Games & Puzzles: board games, card games and jigsaws.
- Outdoor & Active Play: ride-ons, sports sets and backyard toys.
- STEM & Learning: science kits, coding robots and early-literacy toys.

## What is in the site menu at the top of every page?

The header has: Home, Shop all (the full catalogue), STEM, Outdoor, and Feedback. On the right there is a Cart link (with a number badge once the cart has items) and either "Sign in" and "Create account" for visitors, or a button showing the signed-in username. The username menu has My orders, Account and Sign out.

## What is in the footer?

The footer has shortcuts to All toys, Best value (all toys sorted by lowest price first) and Order history, plus a safety reminder: small parts are a choking hazard for children under three, so always read the safety notes before buying. It also states that ToyBox is a demo store and payments run in Stripe test mode.

## Do I need an account to shop?

No. Anyone can browse, fill a cart and check out as a guest without an account. An account adds order history, a saved cart, and the Stripe-based checkout.

## What can the ToyBox assistant help with?

The assistant answers questions about browsing and finding toys, the cart, guest and account checkout, shipping, tax and totals, receipts and order history, creating an account and signing in, and the Feedback page. It cannot look up orders, change accounts or handle refunds; the Feedback page is the place for those requests.
