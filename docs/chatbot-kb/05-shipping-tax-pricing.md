# Shipping, Tax and Pricing

Topic: How shipping, sales tax and the order total are calculated.
Source: Cart page (/cart) and both checkout pages (/checkout, /checkout/guest)

## How much is shipping?

Shipping is a flat $5.99 on any order whose subtotal is under $50.00. Shipping is free when the subtotal is $50.00 or more. An empty cart has no shipping charge.

## How do I get free shipping?

Bring the cart subtotal (the price of the toys before shipping and tax) to $50.00 or more. At exactly $50.00 shipping is already free.

## How much sales tax is charged?

Tax is 6.63% (the New Jersey rate) of the subtotal, rounded to the nearest cent with half a cent rounding up. Shipping is not taxed.

## How is my order total calculated?

Total = subtotal + shipping + tax. Worked example with a $30.00 subtotal: shipping is $5.99 because the subtotal is under $50.00, tax is 6.63% of $30.00 = $1.989, which rounds to $1.99, so the total is $30.00 + $5.99 + $1.99 = $37.98.

A second example with the $94.99 Stargazer Beginner Telescope: shipping is free because the subtotal is over $50.00, tax is 6.63% of $94.99 = $6.2979, which rounds to $6.30, so the total is $94.99 + $0.00 + $6.30 = $101.29.

## Is tax charged on shipping?

No. Tax is worked out on the subtotal only.

## Where do I see shipping, tax and the total before I pay?

The Order summary on the Cart page and the Summary panel in checkout both show Subtotal, Shipping ("Free" or an amount), Estimated tax and Total. The Review step's Pay button also shows the total.

## Can the price in my browser be wrong?

The store recalculates prices, shipping, tax and totals on its server from current prices before payment, and does not trust the numbers sent from the browser. The total at payment is the one that counts.

## Does ToyBox offer discount codes, other shipping speeds or international shipping?

No such options are published on the site. The only shipping rule is the flat $5.99 rate with free shipping at $50.00 or more. Checkout does show a Country field for the account shipping address (pre-filled with US), but ToyBox publishes no international shipping information. Questions about this can go to the Feedback page.

## What currency are prices in?

All prices are in US dollars.
