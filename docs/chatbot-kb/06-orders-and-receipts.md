# Orders and Receipts

Topic: Where signed-in shoppers see their order history, order details and receipts.
Source: /orders, /orders/:orderNumber, and the order confirmation page (/checkout/success)

## How do I see my past orders?

Sign in, open the username menu and choose "My orders" (or use "Order history" in the footer). The page "Your orders" lists your orders with their status. If you have none, it says "No orders yet - When you buy something it will appear here with its status and receipt." with a "Start shopping" button. This page needs a signed-in account.

## What does an order detail page show?

Open an order from the list. The page shows the order number with a status badge, and a timeline of four boxes: Placed, Paid, Shipped and Delivered (each shows a date once reached and is dimmed until then). Below are the items (picture, name, unit price times quantity, line total), the totals (Subtotal, Shipping, Tax, Total), the shipping address and the contact email (labelled Contact email; no receipt is emailed to it), and any payments recorded. A "All orders" link goes back to the list.

## Can I see someone else's order?

No. You can only see your own orders when signed in. If an order cannot be found, the page shows an error and a "Back to orders" button.

## Where is my receipt?

ToyBox does not send receipt emails. Right after paying, the order confirmation page shows the order number, total and items. Afterwards, open any order from My orders to see its full details, which serve as the receipt.

## Will I get a receipt email?

No. There is no emailed receipt. The order details are on the confirmation page and under My orders.

## I did not receive a receipt email. What now?

None is sent, so nothing is lost. Open My orders and choose the order to see its details. The assistant cannot look up an order for you; for anything else, use the Feedback page.

## Can I track my package or check my order status here?

The assistant cannot look up a specific order or its status. Signed-in shoppers can check the status and timeline on their order detail page. For anything else, use the Feedback page.

## Why does my order say pending?

An account-checkout order is pending until the payment is confirmed. After confirmation it is marked paid. If you left the Stripe page without paying, the order stays pending until it is paid. Your cart is untouched in that case.
