# Orders and Receipts

Topic: Where signed-in shoppers see their order history, order details and receipts.
Source: /orders, /orders/:orderNumber, and the guest receipt email

## How do I see my past orders?

Sign in, open the username menu and choose "My orders" (or use "Order history" in the footer). The page "Your orders" lists your orders with their status. If you have none, it says "No orders yet - When you buy something it will appear here with its status and receipt." with a "Start shopping" button. This page needs a signed-in account.

## What does an order detail page show?

Open an order from the list. The page shows the order number with a status badge, and a timeline of four boxes: Placed, Paid, Shipped and Delivered (each shows a date once reached and is dimmed until then). Below are the items (picture, name, unit price times quantity, line total), the totals (Subtotal, Shipping, Tax, Total), the shipping address and receipt email, and any payments recorded. A "All orders" link goes back to the list.

## Can I see someone else's order?

No. You can only see your own orders when signed in. If an order cannot be found, the page shows an error and a "Back to orders" button.

## Where is my receipt?

Signed-in shoppers can open any order from My orders to see its full details, which serve as the receipt. Guest shoppers get an emailed receipt (subject "Your ToyBox order" followed by the order number) listing the items, the total and the shipping address, plus the order confirmation page shown right after paying.

## I did not receive my receipt email. What now?

For guest checkout, the confirmation page tells you whether the email went out. If it said the receipt could not be emailed, the order is still confirmed and the confirmation page is your record. Check the spam folder if the page said it was emailed. The assistant cannot resend a receipt or look up an order; use the Feedback page.

## Can I track my package or check my order status here?

The assistant cannot look up a specific order or its status. Signed-in shoppers can check the status and timeline on their order detail page. For anything else, use the Feedback page.

## Why does my order say pending?

An account-checkout order is pending until the payment is confirmed. After confirmation it is marked paid. If you left the Stripe page without paying, the order stays pending until it is paid. Your cart is untouched in that case.

## Do guest orders appear in an order history?

No. Guest orders are not tied to an account, so they never appear in My orders. Keep the confirmation page details and the emailed receipt.
