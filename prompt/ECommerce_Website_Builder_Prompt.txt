order_items, payments.

category, recommended minimum and maximum age, brand, safety notes, and images.
Business rules to enforce server-side:
- Prices and stock are authoritative on the backend; never trust a price sent by
  the browser.
- Reject checkout when any line item exceeds available stock.
- Decrement stock only when the Stripe webhook confirms payment succeeded.
- Store money as integer cents throughout.
